# Quantity pricing transition

Both preview switches are off by default. Do not enable either on a public production deployment: the transition is incomplete and the buyer flag is **not** an access-control mechanism.

Supplier product creation and editing use the quantity-price editor rather than the legacy grade table. Creating a supplier product atomically saves a validated private pending price; suppliers no longer enter an English name. When an eligible never-published product is submitted for review, the existing AI translator runs if configured. Its write is conditional on the submitted product version and review state, and the CRM reviewer can edit the translation. Translation failure does not prevent submission; the supplier is told staff must complete it. `OPENAI_API_KEY` must be configured for AI. Migrations `20261057` through `20261060` must be applied in order after `20261053`–`20261056`. Without the new RPCs, new creation or submission fails closed. Retries use an idempotency key; pending proposals never replace the previously approved price. A changed product must have a matching price before submission. New-flow products cannot be published before their price is approved and the buyer preview flag is enabled; leave it disabled on public production until the full cutover is ready.

Existing public legacy price fields are deliberately preserved when suppliers or CRM staff edit other product details. The CRM edit form uses the quantity editor; CRM product creation and CSV import still contain legacy pricing paths, and the storefront and other buyer paths still depend on legacy data. These are not dead code and must not be deleted or treated as fully migrated yet. Staff price proposals require an attestation of the supplier's commitment; the audit log is best-effort, not a durable consent ledger.

- `VEXIM_QUANTITY_PRICING_PREVIEW=true` exposes an additional internal supplier editor for approved suppliers and their own eligible products. Submissions write real pending revisions and use the CRM review queue. Use a dedicated staging database and test accounts.
- `VEXIM_QUANTITY_BUYER_PREVIEW=true` makes shared category and ID-based catalog reads use approved quantity-price revisions when present. Home, search, category cards, product detail and comparison use those projected records. Only enable it in an isolated, non-public staging environment.

Only approved revisions are projected; pending revisions never replace them. The existing supplier public-price consent gate still applies. Approved prices remain visible past the configured staleness threshold, but are excluded from category reference ranges and price ranking. If no new approved price exists, the legacy projection is retained. Settings or revision read failures fail closed.

The supplier storefront has a separate legacy projection. Automatic approval of normal prices, AI translation into a separately reviewed editable name for already-published products, product-family cloning, reminders, reconfirmation, promotions and a durable consent history remain incomplete. Existing approved or pending English-name revisions and staff-edited names are preserved and not automatically replaced; the buyer preview still prioritizes an approved name revision over the product translation. Some older English names can still contain legacy grades. Staff must check the English name before publication, particularly if AI was unavailable. Do not cut over production until all buyer paths, naming, CRM/import workflows and end-to-end staging tests are complete.
